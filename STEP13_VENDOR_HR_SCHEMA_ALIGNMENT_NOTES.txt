SECURITY / ACCOUNTING NOTES
---------------------------
- Module actions require an authenticated client-owned vendor context and a
  valid CSRF token, and verify branch/shift/staff ownership before writes.
- New roster and payroll entries are not automatically approved or paid.
- The payroll item form saves draft earnings/deductions only. It does NOT
  calculate South African statutory payroll, generate compliant payslips,
  make payments, or post payroll to the general ledger.
- The messaging UI supports order-linked conversations; it does not yet
  provide unrestricted directory-based chat unrelated to an order.
- Legacy `messages` is retained for older modules; new order-linked messages
  use `cnrbyte_messages`, and new notifications use `cnrbyte_notifications`.
- Internal marketplace refunds and settlement records retain Step 11's limits:
  refund records do not move money back to an external card/bank; settlement
  records document an external payout that must already have occurred.
- GAAP/IFRS-aligned full financial statements and consolidated branch reporting
  have not been certified or fully implemented by this checkpoint.
- Do not treat this ZIP as production-ready until staging database tests,
  browser tests, access-control tests and end-to-end financial reconciliations
  pass.
